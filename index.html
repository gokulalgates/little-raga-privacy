Little Raga — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

This policy applies to the Android app Little Raga on Google Play (package com.learnwithsingam.littleraga).

Short version: Little Raga is made for children learning Carnatic music on the piano. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. A child's practice stays on the device. The microphone, when allowed, is used only on the device and nothing is recorded or uploaded. The optional AI practice coach, which a grown-up must switch on with their own key, sends a short practice summary (no name, no audio) to Anthropic.

Who we are

Little Raga is made by Learn with Singam (Azhagiya Singam). Contact: learnwithsingam@gmail.com.

Information stored on the device

  • The learner's first name or nickname, if a parent enters one.
  • Learning progress: lessons and speeds learnt, practice days, scores in the listening games, the chosen Sa (sruti), the voice range found by the sruti test, and settings.
  • If a grown-up switches on the AI coach: their Anthropic API key and the coach's last notes.

This information is stored only on the device. It is never sent to us. A grown-up can export a learner's progress from the Grown-ups page; the export leaves out the API key. Uninstalling the app erases everything.

Information we collect

We do not collect, sell or share any personal information. The app contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking software.

Microphone

The microphone is optional and is used only when the child turns on singing or real-piano practice. The app listens on the device to tell which note was played or sung. Only pitch readings are kept, in memory, while the activity is open: no sound is recorded, stored or uploaded.

The "little concert" makes an audio file from the notes the child played, synthesized by the app (not a microphone recording). It is shared only if the family chooses to share it, using the phone's own share menu.

Bluetooth

Bluetooth is used only to connect a MIDI piano keyboard, if the family has one. The app does not use Bluetooth to find the device's location and does not ask for location.

Internet use

  • AI practice coach (optional, off by default). A grown-up can switch it on, behind a grown-ups check, with their own Anthropic API key. When the coach is asked for a tip, the app sends Anthropic a short summary of practice measured by the app: swaras often missed, timing and tuning leans, and lessons learnt. No name, no recording and no audio is sent. The request goes from the device directly to Anthropic (api.anthropic.com) under the grown-up's own account; Anthropic's privacy policy applies to it. We never receive it.
  • Purchases. The optional one-time "Full course" purchase is handled entirely by Google Play. We never see payment details. Google's privacy policy applies to the payment.
  • Links. Links for grown-ups (the Learn with Singam YouTube channel and music references) open in the phone's browser, outside the app.

Children's privacy

Little Raga is designed for children and follows Google Play's Families Policy, the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the GDPR. We collect no personal information. Purchases, the AI coach and settings are behind a grown-ups check, and the AI coach sends no personal information.

Changes
